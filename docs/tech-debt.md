# Known technical debt

Small, current, honest. Remove entries when fixed.

| Area | Debt | Why it's OK for now |
|---|---|---|
| map | Soundscape loops play from their loop point only (no intro), and positioned loops get distance falloff but no panning | dust2's positioned loops are distant music |
| map | World reflections take the bumped normal through a screen-space tangent frame (no per-vertex tangents) and sample the cubemap's top mip only; CS:S's highlight is slightly broader (refcmp tunnel_floor_e, abs diff 0.021) | Visually matches; per-vertex tangents if it matters |
| client | Shot effects are placeholders: tracer streaks (speed and frequency guessed) and dark discs for bullet marks, on static surfaces only | Until decals by surface and the games' sprites load |
| games/cs_source | Impact effects (specs/cs_source/impact_effects.md): blood is built from the spec's table of `blood_impact_red_01`, not by reading the `.pcf`; its sprite-card size limits and screen-size fades, the goop's spin stop and yaw flip, and the mist's light tint are left out; particle-system drag is applied per frame as the spec reads it (Q7). The surface colour's base is the texture's average sRGB colour (Q5). Electric-spark flares are depth tested (`$ignorez` not supported) and skip the visibility test. Splash light comes from the lightmap of the surface below. A world hit with no visible surface (sky, nodraw) makes no particles | Spec defaults; the open questions need the game |
| map | Particles blend in linear space (Source blends in gamma), and each material is one mesh sorted against other transparent things by its particles' centre | Small, fast effects; looks right in screenshots |
| weapon | Maps without character models fall back to height bands on the collider for hitgroups; hitboxes follow the client-rate animation, not a per-tick server pose (no lag compensation) | Matches what players see; lag compensation needs a network layer |
| map | Runtime decals: world and props (projected onto the prop's own model triangles, as a child so they move with it), none on characters (blood); lit decals (wood, glass: lightmapped and alpha-blended in the game) drawn as a modulate-style stain; no fog softening; modulate 2x brightening is capped at 2x in linear light (the game's gamma-space 2x can reach about 3x for its lightest texels; an 8-bit target can't hold more in one blend); a 5% edge fade hides atlas bleeding; orientation follows the map decal rule (walls upright, floors along world X; the engine's rule isn't public); limit 512 (the game's is a client setting) | Bullet holes on world surfaces look right; the rest needs observation in the game |
| map | Glow sprite occlusion casts rays at the world, props and brush entities (colliders), not the depth buffer the game's occlusion query uses: props without a collision model don't hide glows | Covers nearly every case; a GPU occlusion query would be exact |
| map | WorldTwoTextureBlend (specs/cs_source/shaders_two_texture_blend.md) follows the public SDK's shader; that CS:S ships the same revision (fixed 2.0 lightmap scale, raw detail) is confirmed only by matching brightness on de_aztec's refcmp views, not by a capture of the shader; mode 0 with `$bumpmap` is undefined | refcmp luma matches to 0.01 on all 13 aztec views |
| games/cs_source | Player animation follows the SDK template (spec §12): ground speeds 100/250/85 make feet slide against CS:S's authored ≈107/150/67; no reload, grenade, death or IK layers | CS:S's own state isn't public; check against a demo (spec open questions) |
| weapon | CS:S's spread uses our own RNG, not Source's seeded generator (spec Q1); the bullet impulse is unmeasured (Q8); no water or lag compensation; armour running out mid-hit is clamped (unmeasured); airborne inaccuracy decays toward the crouched rest with the standing rest as a floor (a fit to M1's jump and no-jump falls); crouched-and-moving recoil uses the moving set and crouched caps the standing ones (unmeasured) | The rest follows the spec's measured values |
| weapon | Penetration (M13): a player's budget cost is a fixed 21.5 units (a fit to the stop cases, Q7), not its thickness; metal and sand damage factors and every other material class (tile, glass, grate, ...: metal's scale 1.0, factor 0.5) are unmeasured guesses; the range halving after players is applied, not after walls (unchecked); the 762MM distance limit is unbounded (passes at 4000, nothing further measured); open surfaces (displacements, anything not a closed solid) stop bullets; the player-just-before-a-wall quirk isn't reproduced. Shots after passing something are `ShotContinued` events: impact sounds, decals and particles mark every surface a bullet enters (the probe logs entry impacts only; exit marks unchecked), but tracers cover only the first segment | Matches every measured damage and pass/stop case the spec lists |
| bot | Bot awareness is ours, not CS:S's (not in the SDK): sight, hearing by each sound's falloff gain, 15 s memory, random roaming; route following is a simple point follower (no gap jumps, ladders untested, no obstacle avoidance); maps without a `.nav` leave bots standing | Plays fairly for deathmatch practice |
| map | View models: near plane 1 cm (not the spec's 1 unit) and their own depth buffer (not a squeezed depth range); no light smoothing or ambient boost; the hands get no reflections (mask in the normal map); muzzle flash look and light falloff, and the shell direction, are our choices from the spec's data (plans/active/view-models.md open questions) | Each needs a CS:S comparison to settle |

| weapon | Guns beyond the AK-47 (M4A1, AWP, USP, Glock, Deagle in `games/cs_source/weapons.rs`): unmeasured parts are marked `UNMEASURED` there: the 556MM and 9MM penetration powers/distances and 45ACP's distance are community values (the same table's 45ACP, 50AE, 762MM and 338MAG powers match M13); the M4A1's moving/airborne kicks are its standing set; semi-automatics kick 2° in every state with no cap; scoped, silenced and burst recoil are the normal ones; silenced damage and range are unchanged (M16 not measured); the Glock's burst refire is 0.5 s; reloading unzooms.
